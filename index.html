核心句型

After + doing

After getting home, I have dinner.

keep + doing

I keep studying English.

focus on + doing

I focus on studying English.

give up + doing

I won't give up studying English.

enough + noun + to do

I have enough time to study.

too + adjective + to do

I'm too tired to study.

was/were going to + verb

I was going to walk my dog.

get ready to + verb

I'm getting ready to cook dinner.
