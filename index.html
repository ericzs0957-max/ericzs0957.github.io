🔥 我的核心句型

After + doing
After watching a movie, I go to the mall.
看完电影后，我去商场。
keep + doing
I want to keep studying English.
我想继续学习英语。
focus on + doing
I want to focus on studying English.
我想专注于学习英语。
give up + doing
I won’t give up studying English.
我不会放弃学习英语。
enough + noun + to do
I have enough time to study English.
too + adjective + to do
I’m too tired to study English.
was/were going to + verb
I was going to walk my dog.
get ready to + verb
I’m going to get ready to cook dinner.

📚 重点词汇

focus 集中注意力 relax 放松 improve 提高 busy 忙的 free 有空的 energy 精力 awake 清醒的 probably 大概 enough 足够 delicious 美味的 usually 通常 rarely 很少

👀 容易混淆

watch / see / look

watch：持续观看

watch a movie

look at：看向某个东西

look at my phone

see：看到 / 见到

see my girlfriend

every day / everyday

every day = 每天

I study English every day.

everyday = 日常的

English is part of my everyday life.

then / than

then = 然后

I have dinner, then I study English.

than = 比

Coffee is better than tea.

Teacher: How are you today?
Leon: I’m feeling great. I have a lot of work today, but it’s not bad.

Leon: I’m not busy now. I have some free time.
