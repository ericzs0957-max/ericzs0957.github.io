🗺️ 我的核心学习地图

After + doing 做完……之后
After getting home, I have dinner.
keep + doing 继续做……
I want to keep studying English.
focus on + doing 专注于做……
I focus on studying English.
give up + doing 放弃做……
I won't give up studying English.
enough + noun + to do 有足够的……去做……
I have enough time to study.
too + adjective + to do 太……而不能……
I'm too tired to study.
was / were going to + verb 原本打算……
I was going to walk my dog.
get ready to + verb 准备做……
I'm getting ready to cook dinner.
